How to read these numbers

  • Residents and households are estimates. A census tract only partly inside the circle contributes in proportion to the share of its area inside it, which assumes people are spread evenly across the tract.
  • IBGE withholds small counts for confidentiality, and this dataset stores them as zero. Sanitation gaps in sparsely populated tracts may therefore be overstated.
  • Physician and nurse counts cover only professionals registered in care teams (mostly primary care). Hospital clinical staff are largely missing, so these are not total workforce figures.
  • Census figures are from the 2022 Demographic Census. Facility figures are from CNES, reference month August 2026.
  • District-level disease, birth and mortality indicators (SINAN, SINASC, SIM) are not loaded yet, so they are not shown.
